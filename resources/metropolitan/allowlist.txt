# Terms the automatic gates in scripts/theme_tier.py cannot get right, and why.
#
# The gates reject a candidate when a national reference corpus says it is common
# (CSTenTen score >= 41) or when MorphoDiTa reads it as a personal name. Both are
# correct in general and wrong for one specific, small class: the words a
# publication owns outright. Brno is nationally common *because* it is a city of
# 400 000 people, and it is still the single most Brno word there is. Masna and
# Prygl parse as surnames because Czech street and lake nicknames look like them.
#
# Every publication needs a list like this and it is the only irreducibly manual
# part of the tier. It stays short: the city's own name and its cases, the local
# university initialisms, and the landmark nicknames locals use instead of the
# official name. Roughly forty entries, written once per publication, not per issue.
